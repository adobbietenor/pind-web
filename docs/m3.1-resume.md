# M3.1 — where it stopped, for the next session

Branch **`phase3/m3.1-identity`**, pushed, **not merged**. 34 commits and **20
migrations** on top of `main`, all applied to pind-staging. Deployed to pind.social.

**Green:** `test:policies` **91 of 91** (read from its own exit code, not through a
pipe), `test:unit` **305 of 305**, typecheck clean on the Worker, `packages/shared`
and the app.

**One thing stands between this and the merge: the photo walk on a device** (below).
Everything else on the acceptance list has passed on a phone. The Done entry is drafted
at the bottom of this file, ready to land once the walk passes.

Read `CLAUDE.md` first. This milestone added four rules that explain most of what went
wrong and why: **"An instrument that is wrong in a way that looks like a finding"**
(and, beside it, *read a disputed setting from the thing that enforces it, not the
thing that displays it*), **"A guard that never ran looks exactly like a guard that
passed"**, **"A test of a rule proves nothing about a screen that does not call it"**,
and **never run `supabase config push`**. Every decision's reasoning is in
`decisions.md` from "Decided in Phase 3 M3.1" onward.

## The walk that remains — and it needs a new build first

**Walked (Alex, on the `bbe840f` build):** Change photo → Choose another → Save, and
Remove photo → Save — both pass. **The airplane-mode walk failed before it reached the
upload**: A2 said *"We lost your sign-in. Go back and sign in again"*, with a session
that was fine and no button to go anywhere. Every screen asked `auth.getUser()`, a
network call whose offline answer is `user: null` plus an error nobody read. Fixed
everywhere it was shared (A2, A3, Change photo, Profile, Edit tags, Settings,
`loadMe`): who you are is read from the device (`lib/session.ts`), classified by
`readSession` in `@pind/shared` (N01–N09: offline is never "out"), and every failure
sentence renders through `Trouble`, which carries its exit — **Sign in again** for a
real sign-out, **Try again** for the rest (S16–S19 fail on the old code).

**The TestFlight build is from `7ecb127`** (Alex started it), and the web was deployed
from the same commit so the two match during the walk — the offline fix is in the app
bundle, so the `bbe840f` build still has the old check.

```

npx eas-cli@24.7.0 build --profile internal --platform ios
```

If EAS offers to revoke a distribution certificate, **the answer is no** (the Tenor
team is shared).

Then, on the phone, signed in to an account **that already has a photo**:

1. **Profile → Change photo → Choose another → Save.** The new photo shows on the
   profile. **Then Change photo → Remove photo → Save.** The profile has no photo, and

2. **The forced upload failure.** Profile → **Change photo first, while online** (the
   screen loads your current photo on the way in). *Then* turn on **airplane mode and
   turn Wi-Fi off** (grey in Control Center, not blue). Choose a photo **taken on the
   phone** (one that lives only in iCloud fails at the picker instead), then tap
   **Save**. The session is now read from the phone, so nothing stops before the
   upload, and the upload is what fails. Expect:
   - the new pick **greyed out**;
   - the sentence *"We could not upload your photo — Pin'd could not be reached. You
     can choose another, or remove it and continue without one; you can add a photo
     later."* — **no hostname and no error text** (the web walk printed "load failed
     (mxuajvlrkggrqpntekqt.supabase.co)"; only sentences we wrote reach a screen now,
     and the raw error goes to Sentry — said.ts, L01–L07, S20–S21). The web and the
     build are both from `7ecb127`;
   - **Remove photo** discards the failed pick and **the old photo comes back**.
3. **Offline says offline, with a way out.** Still in airplane mode with Wi-Fi off:
   - go back to the **Profile** tab (switch tabs and back): *"Pin'd could not be
     reached. Check your connection and try again — you have not been signed out, and
     nothing here was lost."* with **Try again** — not "Nothing here yet — set up your
     profile";
   - Profile → **Edit tags**: the same sentence with **Try again** and **Back to your
     profile** — not an empty picker;
   - turn the network back on and tap **Try again** on each: the screen loads.

That is the acceptance line "A2's photo path is walked on both platforms, including a
failed upload removed" — the path that only ever failed on the phone.
