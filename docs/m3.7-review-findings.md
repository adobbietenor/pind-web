Reviewer: a fresh adversarial session, 29 Oct 2026, against pind-staging, on branch
`phase3/issue-log`. Brief: `docs/m3.7-review-brief.md`. I also attacked the changes made
after the brief: reports on people (`20261016153531`, `20261016160255`), `my_chats` (the five
`20261016190356`…`195609` files), the thread purge (`20261016211217`), and
`app/src/lib/myevents.ts` with `app/src/app/(tabs)/my-events.tsx`.

All reproductions are in **`tests/policies/review-m3.7.test.ts`**. It is not in `test:policies`.
Run it with:

    node --env-file=.dev.vars --test --test-concurrency=1 tests/policies/review-m3.7.test.ts

I ran it twice, with the same result both times: **17 cases, 9 pass, 8 fail.**
- **Failing cases (`-F`)** are findings. Each one should pass once the problem is fixed.
- **Passing cases (`-H`)** are attacks that held.
- **Passing cases (`-D`)** record behaviour that follows from a rule as written. They are
  for Alex to decide, not leaks.

The run removes everything it creates. Its `after` hook asserts that 0 people, gatherings,
venues and users are left from the run, and it uses `removeUser` rather than the sweep, so
the other reviewer's rows are not touched. No case sends a name to the AI: every person is a
harness user, and the two calls to the Worker cannot reach the model.

The service key was used for:
- setup and teardown;
- making time pass (a gathering moved to last night, a squad set to `done` as the
  lifecycle job would set it);
- one admin hide, the same column H9's auto-hide sets;
- RV05, whose subject is the claim that seats are held "even against the service key".

Every leak was reproduced with a signed-in person's own token.

## Findings

### F1. A hidden person's messages stay in a squad's thread. Leak, reproduced.

- **Rule broken:** V20/V10 as applied to the room: "A hidden person's messages become
  unreadable to everyone but their author at the moment of the hide". V21's harassment
  remedy, "block + report + a moderation hide, which removes that person". Q39.
- **Cause:** `crew_messages_read_members` filters only on the message's own `hidden_at`. It
  never checks whether the author is hidden. The hide trigger takes the person out of the
  squad but leaves their words.
- **Why it matters more now:** joining is one tap and members read the thread from the
  start. So every stranger who joins later reads what the hidden person wrote.
- **Reproduction (`RV08-F`):**
  1. Hid posts "hid was here" in squad s3.
  2. Hid is hidden. He leaves the squad, which is correct.
  3. Cyd, a member, still reads the message.
  4. Xav joins after the hide and reads it from the start.
  5. Cyd's A19 `my_chats` preview is "hid was here", and it counts as unread.
- **Would the harness catch it?** No. P251 proves this rule for the room's preview only.
  P233 checks that the hidden person leaves the squad, not what happens to their words.

### F2. Hidden after the night, a person stays in a done squad and can read and post for 24 hours. Leak, reproduced.

- **Rule broken:** §12m: "A hidden person — cannot stay in any squad (H9 or an admin hide
  takes them out)". Q39 / V10 §11: "a hidden person sees no one, from a group … too".
- **Cause:**
  - `private.people_hidden_leave_squads` only acts on squads in `spot_set` or `live`.
  - `crew_messages_post_members` does not check `is_hidden`.
- **The gap:** after the night, a person reported "uncomfortable" (hidden at once) keeps
  reading the thread. They can also post into it, to the people who reported them, until
  24 hours after the end.
- **Reproduction (`RV09-F`):**
  1. Hid is a member of squad sD, which is `done`, at gathering D (ended an hour ago).
  2. Hid is hidden. He stays a member.
  3. He reads the thread.
  4. His post "still here, hidden" is accepted, and Xav reads it.
- **Would the harness catch it?** No. P233 hides someone in a `spot_set` squad only.

### F3. Someone who stops being eligible stays in a women-only squad. Leak, reproduced.

- **Rule broken:** §12m: "a women-only squad is for women-only-eligible people only". The
  decision says a women-only squad "doesn't exist for anyone ineligible … whatever the
  request".
- **Cause:**
  - Eligibility is checked only at join time.
  - `gender` and `include_in_women_only` can be updated by the owner. This is the write
    that `app/src/lib/profile.ts` `saveYou` makes from Profile.
  - Nothing reacts to a change in either column. The only trigger is on `women_only_rooms`.
- **Reproduction (`RV06-F`):**
  1. Wyn (woman) joins Wen's women-only squad.
  2. Wyn updates her own `people_private` to `gender = 'man'`.
  3. The squad correctly disappears from her list.
  4. But she is still an active member, and she reads its thread.
- The same gap probably exists for the women-only room. I did not test that.
- **Would the harness catch it?** No. P232 tests the decision at join time only.

### F4. A race between "women-only rooms only" and a join leaves her in a mixed squad. Leak (a race), reproduced.

- **Rule broken:** §12m: someone with "women-only rooms only" cannot "see or join a mixed
  squad; stay in one when they switch it on".
- **Cause:**
  - `join_squad` reads `women_only_only` in `can_see_squad`, then inserts.
  - `women_only_leaves_mixed_squads` runs in the other transaction. It only sees rows that
    were committed before it ran.
  - No lock is shared between the two. (`join_squad` locks the crew row; the switch locks
    nothing on it.)
- **Reproduction (`RV13-F`):** fire `join_squad(s3)` and `set_women_only_rooms(true)` at the
  same time from Rae's session. It reproduced on attempt 5 in the first run and attempt 2 in
  the second. Afterwards Rae is an active member of a mixed squad with
  `women_only_rooms = true`.
- The same race very likely exists for:
  - a block against a join (`blocks_blocker_leaves_group`);
  - a hide against a join (`people_hidden_leave_squads`).

  Both have the same shape: the trigger only sees committed members. I did not run those two.
- **Would the harness catch it?** No. The harness runs sequentially.

### F5. Eight seats can be exceeded by raising your party size after joining. Robustness, reproduced.

- **Rule broken:** decision: "up to 8 people including whoever made it … +1s counted";
  §12m: "join one that's full (8 seats …)".
- **Cause:** seats are fixed at join from `least(party_total, 2)`. A later pin edit (the
  ordinary `pins_update_own`, with `party_total` granted) changes nothing.
- **Reproduction (`RV04-F`):**
  1. The squad is full: 8 people, each with 1 seat.
  2. Bob updates his own pin to `party_total = 2`.
  3. Nine people are now coming. The squad still says 8 of 8, and `squads()` shows
     `seats = 8`.
- **Would the harness catch it?** No. P231 sets +1s before the join only.

### F6. "Held even against the service key" has a hole: moving a member row between squads. Robustness, reproduced.

- **Cause:** the trigger `crew_members_squad_eight_seats` fires on `insert or update of
  left_at, seats`, but not on `update of crew_id`.
- **Reproduction (`RV05-F`):**
  1. The service key updates Zed's membership row from squad s2 to the full squad s1.
  2. It is accepted. s1 now holds 9 seats.
  3. The control (an insert into s1) is refused.
- No session can reach this: there is no update grant on `crew_id`. It matters only because
  §12m and P231 claim the seats hold against the service key.
- **Would the harness catch it?** No. P231 tests the insert only.

### F7. A refused name can be resubmitted unchanged, so the creator can re-roll the AI. Robustness (bypasses the name check), reproduced.

- **Cause:** `rename_squad` accepts any 1–40 character name while the status is `pending` or
  `rejected`, including the same rejected name. It sets the status back to `pending`, which
  fires the webhook, and the model judges it again.
- **Consequence:** the check runs at low effort and gives non-deterministic verdicts. A
  harmful name that is rejected most of the time will eventually pass if the creator keeps
  resubmitting it. Trivial variants (a trailing emoji, a full stop) work the same way.
- **Reproduction (`RV14-F`):**
  1. "Something awful" is recorded as `rejected`.
  2. Wen renames it to "Something awful".
  3. The call is accepted and the status is `pending` again.
  4. For a real person, that would be another AI call.
- **Would the harness catch it?** No. P235 renames to a different name.

### F8. Renaming has no limit, so one person can spend the city's daily AI cap. Robustness, reproduced (the spend reasoned from code).

- **Rule broken:** decisions: the name check is "counted in the daily cap". CLAUDE.md, AI
  calls: "a per-run budget and the daily cap".
- **Reproduction (`RV15-F`):** 25 renames in a row while the name is pending were all
  accepted. For a real (non-harness) creator, each rename changes `name` and so fires
  `crews_squad_name_webhook`, which makes one AI call. There is no rate limit on
  `rename_squad`, or on `start_squad` followed by `leave_group` followed by `start_squad`
  again. The last one out dissolves the squad, so the loop can repeat.
- **What happens at the cap:**
  - The cap is `AI_DAILY_CAP_USD`, default $3, at about $0.005 a call. That is roughly
    600–1,500 renames.
  - The cap is shared by the photo check, the community check and the nightly import
    (`admin_ai_spend_today`).
  - So one opted-in person can switch off photo checks, the import's AI and every other
    squad's name check for the rest of the day.
- **Not run against the AI on purpose.** The count above is from code. The harness's squads
  skip the webhook.
- **Two smaller spend issues, from code only:**
  - Every start and rename makes **two** AI calls. The database webhook and the app's own
    `/squad-name-check` (`checkNameSoon`) both fire within milliseconds, both re-read the
    name as `pending`, and both call the model. The second verdict is a no-op, but its cost
    is recorded. This is the same shape as the photo check's webhook plus net, so it is
    probably pre-existing.
  - `canSpend` is checked before the spend. A burst can therefore overshoot the cap by the
    number of calls already in flight.
- **Would the harness catch it?** No.

## Design observations: Alex's call, not leaks (reproduced, passing `-D` cases)

### D1. "Counted, not drawn" lasts one tap (`RV10-D`). Inference.

- Off joined s3 and then turned "open to meeting" off. The card no longer names him.
- Rae taps Join, and through `after_peer`'s live-group branch she reads Off's name and photo
  path. She leaves, and loses them again.
- Joining twice in a row (join, read, leave) can harvest every member of every squad, and
  every thread from the start, including members the people list would not show.
- This matches "A member: read … its members". It is worth knowing that open join makes it
  a one-tap lookup for strangers.
- Nothing takes a member out of a squad when they turn meeting off or remove their pin.

### D2. After the night, open join is a 7-day key (`RV11-D`). Inference.

- Xav joined sD at the last minute. He never ticked "we met" and has no connection.
- After the night he reads Cyd's people row, and `after_state` lists Cyd with her photo, for
  V23's 7 days.
- This is V23 as written ("a member of a finished group for its 7 days"). But V23 was
  written when a group meant mutual invitation. With open join, anyone can get a week of a
  stranger's profile by tapping Join just before the squad meets.
- Connections still need mutual ticks, so no connection, handle or #7 path opens.

### D3. One person, one safety report, delists a squad for everyone (`RV17-D`).

- "Uncomfortable" from anyone a squad is listed to hides it at once. That is H9's letter.
  So one person can delist every squad at a gathering, one tap each, with no cost to them.
- A member can do the same to their own squad: the policy's `in_group` branch. That closes
  it to joiners and leaves an unfounded safety report in the admin queue.
- Below H9 it held: a single "spam" report does not hide a squad (P236).
- Reports from a squad's messages let any member instantly hide any author they can read.
  That includes members who are off the people list.

## Suspected, not reproduced

- **A day-of push for a withdrawn gathering (from code).** `admin_groups_tick` sets squads
  `live` whether or not the gathering is withdrawn. `notify_group_change`'s live branch then
  writes "Tonight: … — tap when you're there". The decision says a withdrawn gathering's
  squad stays "for them to talk it over". A push that sends people to a spot for a cancelled
  event looks wrong. I did not run the global tick to prove it.
- **Escaping the name's fence (from code).** `squadNameRequest` puts the name between
  `<name>` and `</name>` without escaping, and `rename_squad` / `start_squad` refuse only
  control characters. So a name can contain `</name>` and close the fence early.
  - The JSON-schema output and the 40-character limit leave little room for an injection.
  - F7's re-roll is a far cheaper way to get a harmful name through.
  - I made no AI calls to test it.
- **The harness promise has a gap (from code).** P235 says "The harness's squads never go to
  the AI", but that is true only of the webhook and the sweep. `/squad-name-check` does not
  check `is_harness_user`. A harness member calling it on their own pending squad would
  spend.
- **Timing cannot be tested from a client.** In `join_squad`, a block, a hidden squad, a
  women-only squad and a past squad are all refused at `can_see_squad`, before the seat
  count. A full squad is refused after `in_a_group_at` and two seat sums. The difference is
  sub-millisecond database work, well below HTTP jitter, and the words and hint are
  identical (P231 and P232 assert this). I judge it not usable as an oracle. The only
  difference in errors is `squad_in_group` against `squad_closed` for someone already in a
  group. It reveals only whether `can_see_squad` holds, which `squads()` already reveals.

## Attacked and found holding

**Doors**
- `private` is not an exposed schema. `can_see_squad`, `in_group`, `my_seen_in_crew` and
  `may_report_from_a_message` refuse a session (`RV01-H`).
- `start_group`, `invite_more`, `respond_to_invite`, `my_invites`, `squads`, `join_squad` and
  `my_chats` all refuse a visitor (`RV02-H`).
- A legacy group with an approved name, open state and future time is never listed, never
  joinable, and not reportable as a squad (`RV02-H`).

**Seats**
- Three sessions racing for the eighth seat: exactly one gets it, and the others get
  `squad_closed` (`RV03-H`). The crew-row lock is held, and each statement takes a fresh
  snapshot under read committed.
- Joining twice, rejoining after leaving, and the service-key insert of a ninth seat were
  all covered by P231. I re-checked the insert as RV05's control.

**Hidden people and reports**
- A hidden person cannot rejoin (`squad_closed`) (`RV12-H`).
- Someone who has left a squad cannot report anyone from its thread: the message subquery
  runs under `crew_messages_read_members` (`RV12-H`).

**The name check**
- `/squad-name-check` on the deployed Worker refuses a non-member with 404 before any check,
  and a member of an approved squad gets `checked: false`. No `app` check row was written
  (`RV16-H`).
- **From code:**
  - The webhook needs the shared secret, compared trimmed on both sides.
  - It re-reads the row with the service key.
  - `admin_record_squad_name_check` moves only a still-pending name that is still the name
    it judged.
  - Nothing a session can call writes `name_status` (P229).
  - Renaming an approved name is refused (P235).
  - There is no path from `approved` back to an editable state.

**Blocks**
- I re-read `can_see_squad` (a block either way with any active member makes the squad
  absent), `squads()`, `my_chats.open_squads` (which is `squads()`) and the identical
  refusal words. I found no count, hint or listing difference that reveals a block beyond
  what V4's mutual invisibility already shows.
- Seats count everyone, but a squad with a blocked member is absent altogether, so the
  count cannot be compared.

**Handles**
- `share_crew` now requires `kind = 'solo'`, so a squad grants no handle (P234).
- Squad membership creates no `connections` row. `keep_in_touch` still needs mutual
  `we_met` ticks, so there is no #7 path from a squad alone.

**`my_chats` (from code, and P248–P253)**
- It is SECURITY INVOKER, so every read goes through the existing policies.
- `last_seen_at` has no column grant. The table grant was revoked and re-granted per column.
- The seen-time helpers answer only for `private.me()`.
- Joining from My Events is the same `join_squad` RPC as A15, with nothing added in the app.
- Its weakness is F1: it inherits the thread rule's blind spot for hidden authors.

**Reports on people (16 Oct)**
- The context must name a real `user` message by that author, at that exact time, that the
  reporter can read under RLS.
- A hidden reporter, a report on yourself and a report across a block are refused by
  `may_report_from_a_message`.
- A plain person report cannot carry a forged context.
- H9's crew branch counts distinct reporters (P236 held at one spam report).

**Q11 purge**
- `admin_delete_expired_crew_messages` is executable only by `service_role`. It uses the
  room's rule (effective end + 24 h + 30 days, computed live, so a moved date moves it).
  Report rows outlive the messages (`context_crew_id` / `target_message_id` are set to null).
  P254 covers both sides.
- I did not check from here that the pg_cron job is registered on staging. The service key
  cannot read `cron.job` through the API.

**Retirement (from code)**
- `crew_proposals` has no session insert, and the vote policy needs `state = 'forming'`,
  which squads never have.
- `crew_invites` is now read only by `admin_groups_tick`'s expiry and the read-own policy.
