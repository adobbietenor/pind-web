# M3.1 handover — what the last two days decided that touches identity and the app

Read `CLAUDE.md`, `spec.md` §2/§3/§5/§6, `decisions.md` Part 5 and `docs/build-plan.md`
§8 M3.1 as usual. This is the short version of what changed under M3.1's feet while
Phase 2 was built, so the session does not rediscover it or contradict it.

**The acceptance list is `docs/build-plan.md` §8 M3.1**, and it is the session's first
message.

---

## 1 · What the database already does, so M3.1 does not rebuild it

- **The reciprocal list already works at two people.** `private.can_see_at` never
  mentions five: it asks only that both people opted in at that gathering, that the list
  is open, and that neither has blocked the other. **Only crews opening and the
  gender-mix chip are gated at the threshold** (V3, Q3). So "what the locked state
  offers" is a screen decision with nothing owed in the data layer — the database is
  already on the side we want.
- **Anonymous at pin, permanent at opt-in, same user id.** `people.auth_user_id` is
  nullable, anonymous sign-ins are on for pind-staging, and the link (`updateUser` with
  an email, `linkIdentity` for OAuth) keeps the pin, the party size and the opt-in.
- **Gender and birth year are already owner-only**, in `people_private` (D1). Gender is
  asked once and appears on no profile, not even your own. Birth year only, never a date.
- **Seed rows are invisible to every visitor, signed in or out** (V18). The policy
  harness's own people are *not* seeds — that is deliberate and documented — so a new
  test person should follow the harness's convention rather than inventing one.
- **V17 is stricter than the schema is today, and that is M3.1's job.**
  `instagram_handle` sits on the `people` row, so it is currently readable by anyone V1
  allows. It must move off the row behind its own rule: crewmates, a solo-plan partner
  and connections can read it; the open "going & open to meeting" list, a blocked
  person, a hidden person and `anon` cannot. RLS hides rows, not columns — this is a
  migration and a policy, not a screen decision, and it needs harness cases proving both
  sides.
- **V6 still says a photo shows only after *admin* approval.** M3.1's AI check replaces
  that with three outcomes, and V6 has to be rewritten in the same milestone, with
  `moderation_log` recording each automated decision under the actor `ai:photo-check`. A
  rejected photo leaves the person **visible without one**.

## 2 · Copy and screen decisions that bind A1–A3, A9 and A10

- **The house rules were rewritten by Alex** after the M2.1 on-device walk and are in
  `packages/shared` (`HOUSE_RULES`, `CREWS_MEET`). The safety property each line
  describes is unchanged; only how it is said. They are verbatim on every crowd surface
  and are what App Review is pointed at under Guideline 1.2. Never retyped, never
  softened.
- **The threshold never leads anywhere.** W2 is headed **"Who else is going?"** with the
  counts beneath it and `THRESHOLD_EXPLANATION` as a quiet grey line under those. **The
  pinned page leads with "Find your crew"** (Alex), where forming one is genuinely the
  next action. A1–A3 and A9/A10 must not open on the number.
- **A card is the count and the invitation, nothing else**: "0 pinned · be the first" or
  "3 pinned · see who's going". Crew state belongs on the page you land on, not on the
  row that gets you there.
- **One button everywhere: "Pin in — I'm going"**, whatever it costs to get in, with the
  cost on its own line beneath and any registration line above. `PIN_IN` is a single
  constant; "I've got a ticket" is retired.
- **A8 mirrors W2's anatomy**, and W2 has moved: a reader-facing line about what the
  gathering is, numbered spot cards with the walking-directions link **inside the card**
  (a spot is a card, not a maps link), "Open in Maps" for the venue, and the map itself a
  static picture at a zoom chosen per venue.
- **Entry is three states, not two.** `is_free` is gone: `entry` is free / door /
  ticketed with `door_price_cents` and `entry_note`. Never say free unless it is free,
  and an unknown door price reads "pay at the door" rather than nothing.
- Dark only, both platforms, in both system settings. Poppins headlines, system body.
  Design tokens, fixed copy and constants come from `packages/shared`, never retyped.

## 3 · What M3.1 owns that is expensive to get wrong

- **The tags.** `TAGS_DRAFT` in `packages/shared` is **20 tags in four groups** and is
  Tatiana's to reword until the seed. M3.1 owns both the final list and the seed
  migration, and **after the seed a change costs a data migration**, because
  `person_tags` points at tag slugs. Do not confuse these with W1's category chips:
  those are a browse aid on *gatherings* and have no relationship to a person's tags.
- **The photo check follows the M1.3b call pattern** by default: streamed, aborted
  outright after four minutes, at most one retry, a per-run budget and the daily cap,
  and **an estimate counted for every aborted call**, because an aborted call is billed
  and its usage never arrives.
- **Its spend must land where the cap can see it.** `public.admin_ai_spend_today` now
  sums **two** tables (`import_runs` and `community_check_runs`) because a second job's
  cost was invisible to the $3/day cap — that is the shape of the bug, and a third job
  that records its spend nowhere turns a hard cap into a suggestion.
- **Measure before shipping, twice, with a control set.** The rule the comedy rubric
  established: a change to a rubric or threshold is judged by running it twice — once
  with the current wording, to find the noise floor — against a labelled set, and it
  ships together with a re-score of whatever it has already judged. For the photo check
  that means a small labelled set of real photos (a clear face, a group, a cartoon, a
  screenshot of someone else, something inappropriate) run before it is trusted, with
  **the uncertain outcome measured too** — not only the pass and the fail.
- **The two sentences must not converge.** "We could not tell" and "this was rejected"
  are different states and must read differently wherever they appear, the same way the
  liveness check distinguishes a page it cannot read from a series that has stopped. In
  the photo queue this is the difference between "needs a human" and "refused".
- **A visitor is never the thing that does the work** (CLAUDE.md). The check runs from
  the database webhook or a job; the app's direct call to the Worker after upload is the
  net, not the mechanism.
- **Two questions before any job does work**: *which rows are public* —
  `publicVenueIds()`/`crowds()` in `src/public/data.ts`, read as a visitor so RLS
  answers — and *which rows are current* — `readerFloor()`/`daysAfterFloor()` in
  `src/public/list.ts`. Both exist because three separate jobs each invented their own
  answer and each was wrong in a way nothing noticed. `tests/unit/door.test.ts` fails the
  build if the first one is hand-rolled again.
- **Any new Worker route goes into `run_worker_first` in `wrangler.jsonc` in the same
  commit.** A1–A3 are app routes and need nothing; but a missing Worker route does not
  404, it silently serves the app's own `index.html` with a 200 and then gets cached.

## 4 · Owed elsewhere — M3.1 should not solve these

- **The nudge** — a preset, plan-shaped line ("Shall we start a crew?") that opens a
  free-text thread on a one-tap accept — is **M3.3**, and solo (M3.4) reuses it. Building
  solo's version first and retrofitting crews is the expensive order.
- **How a crew convenes** is a property of the gathering (`at_the_gathering` /
  `a_spot_first` / `after`, defaulting by source) and is **M3.3**.
- **Closing the pinning window at the effective end** is **M3.2**, and P37b is inverted
  rather than treated as a regression.
- **Search** (designed, ~2 h, one door function shared with the app's list), the
  **Lighthouse run and request list** (deliberately deferred from M2.3, where there was
  nothing for it to find) and the **tab icons** are all **M3.2**.
- **The full copy pass with Tatiana** is owed before the first real crowds. Anything
  written meanwhile is in the new voice — an invitation, not a safety notice — except the
  house rules, which are fixed.
- **The chip names are owed** ("Community" as a tab name; cycling and running as
  separate chips). Whatever replaces them must keep the measured fact that running is
  4 gatherings / 59 rows / 2 venues while cycling is 5 / 13 / 8 — they look alike and
  behave oppositely.

## 5 · The state of the world M3.1 starts from

Phase 2 is complete. pind.social serves W1–W4 from pind-staging with two tabs, chips, a
weekly pager, reader-facing lines and real venue maps; the nightly import scores,
categorises, describes, publishes and renders maps; a second cron reads 28 community
series' own pages weekly. 206 unit tests, 68 policy-harness cases, five M2.3 migrations.
The app is still the M2.0 shell: four empty tabs, dark, no icons, anonymous sign-in wired
but never called on launch.
