## Decisions taken in M3.2 (full reasoning in decisions.md)

- **A26 on the Worker; the boundary rule restated** — everything before commitment is the
  Worker, including the form that commits; the Worker creates an anonymous session once and
  hands it over by a sealed cookie the app claims with `setSession`. Measured, not assumed.
- **The ruler** for A26 is W2 on the same Lighthouse profile, confirmed by eye on a cold phone.
- **The testers list** — for walking a list with test people; on M4.2's review.
- **The opt-in gate** — open to meeting only after A27. "1 of 5" on Alex's walk: the count
  was honest about the flag, and the flag was wrong.
- **The merge** — nothing moves until the code proves the address; the service-key step only
  with both sessions together; each check proven by a firing test; a failure mid-way says
  "Nothing was lost — your pin is still there".
- **After the pin** — an instant "You're in" with one primary button into the app, the app
  preloading behind it; ticked "meet up" → A27, not ticked → A9.
- **The copy** — "open to meeting" is the only word for the second number ("Crews form once
  5 people are open to meeting."); "going" for the count; "You're in" not "You're #3
  pinned"; the profile's count reads "gatherings". W1's card left for Alex (it would say
  "going" twice).
- **A +1 is never counted as open to meeting** — considered and rejected; do not revisit
  without a new reason.
- **Two pins after the merge: the last one wins**; after the effective end a pin can be
  removed, not edited; **under 19 at A27 removes everything**.
- **Privacy**: operator Tenor Investments Inc., 2562 136th Street, Surrey, BC V4P 1S4;
  Privacy Officer Alex at privacy@pind.social; BC law; PIPA and PIPEDA both addressed;
  data in ca-central-1; `pind-prod` in ca-central-1 is a hard M4.3 requirement.
- **The steer** — the app is the product, the web the on-ramp; after-pin screens are judged
  on the iPhone first and checked once in iPhone Safari.
- **M3.2b** split out; M3.3 stays the loop.
