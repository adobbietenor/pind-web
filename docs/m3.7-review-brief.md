# Review brief — M3.7: squads (A9, A11, A15)

For an **independent adversarial review**: a fresh Claude Code session with no prior
context, tasked only with finding leaks (decisions.md Part 5). Squads are the first group
**anyone open to meeting can join**, and the first free text a stranger broadcasts at
scale. Attack both.

Alex has agreed the rules in plain English: `docs/visibility.md` §12m, and decisions "M3.7:
named squads, open to join". Your job:
- find any way a visitor, a signed-in person, or someone holding their own session token
  can read, infer, join or change what those rules say they must not;
- say whether the tests would catch it.

Report each leak with the rule it breaks and a concrete reproduction; a failing harness
case is best. **Do not fix anything**: fixes come afterwards as new migrations, each with
its case. Work against pind-staging only, never production.

## What to attack

- **Women-only, by any door.** Can an ineligible person see, count, join or read a
  women-only squad?
  - `squads`, `join_squad` by id, `crews`, `crew_members` and the thread;
  - the people and photo reads (V1, V6), `after_peer` afterwards (V23), A16;
  - a notification.

  Can someone with "women-only rooms only" end up in a mixed squad (a race between the
  switch and a join, or a squad made before they switched)?
- **Blocks.** Can a blocked person learn a squad exists, who is in it, or that they were
  blocked:
  - from a count (`seats`, the card's members), a timing, a refusal's words or hint;
  - from which squads are full;
  - from `squads` versus the people list?
- **Eight seats.** Race joins. Join twice. Re-join after leaving. Change `party_total`
  after joining (seats are taken at join). Use the service key path (the trigger).
- **The name.**
  - Can anything list a name before it is approved?
  - Can a person approve their own, or change it after approval?
  - Can a renamed squad keep its old approval?
  - Can a crafted name steer the check (it goes in fenced; try anyway)?
  - Can the check be made to run on someone else's squad (`/squad-name-check` reads as
    the caller)?
  - Can the sweep or the webhook be made to spend without limit?
- **Membership as a key.** Joining is now one tap. What does a member get?
  - Is it exactly the thread, the members and "I'm here"?
  - Not handles (`share_crew`).
  - Not anything from V23 beyond what a squadmate of the night should have: does open
    join turn A16 and `after_peer` into a way to keep seeing strangers for 7 days?
  - Not a `connections` row, and not a #7 invite path.
- **Hidden and reported.**
  - A hidden person leaves every squad: is there a way back in, or a way to stay?
  - Can one person's reports hide a squad below H9?
  - Can a member report their own squad to hide it from others?
- **Retirement.**
  - Are `start_group`, `invite_more`, `respond_to_invite` and `my_invites` closed?
  - Can a legacy group be listed or joined?
  - Does anything still read `crew_invites` in a way that matters?

## Calls Alex made that you should test rather than assume

- Handles are not shown in squads; a 1-on-1 partner and connections only.
- Joins and leaves notify nobody (until the copy pass).
- A withdrawn gathering keeps its squads for their members, off the list.
- A squad never dissolves for being small; the last one out ends it.
