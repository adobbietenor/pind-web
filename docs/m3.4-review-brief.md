# Review brief — M3.4: 1-on-1 (A28, A29)

For an **independent adversarial review**: a fresh Claude Code session with no prior
context, tasked only with finding leaks (decisions.md Part 5; Alex, 14 Oct 2026: "flag it
for the independent review so it gets attacked properly rather than assumed").

Alex has agreed the rules in plain English: `docs/visibility.md` §12k, and decisions
"M3.4: who can see whom in 1-on-1" and "M3.4: the 1-on-1 lifecycle". Your job:
- find any way a visitor, a signed-in person, or someone holding their own session token
  can read, infer or change what those rules say they must not;
- say whether the tests would catch it.

Report each leak with the rule it breaks and a concrete reproduction; a failing harness
case is best. **Do not fix anything**: fixes come afterwards as new migrations, each
with its case. Work against pind-staging only, never production.

1-on-1 is **the highest-risk state in the product** (Alex): two people, a place and a
time. Treat any inference as a leak. That includes a decline, a block, a report or
someone's women-only choice, inferred from a count, a timing, an error message, a
notification or the export.

## What to attack

- **Sender inference.** Can a sender tell a decline from no answer before the start?
  Ways in:
  - `my_solo_proposals`, the open count, or what `propose_solo` refuses;
  - the export (`my_solo_export`);
  - timing, or the notifications table;
  - what happens to a declined proposal when the recipient accepts someone else, blocks,
    is reported, or switches off.
- **Reason inference.** Can anyone learn why someone vanished from their 1-on-1 list?
  The possible reasons: a block, a report (`hidden_from_solo`), a hide, women only, their
  switch, or a plan with someone else. Compare the refusal words and the error codes.
- **Women only, by any door:**
  - `solo_people`, `propose_solo` and `respond_to_solo`;
  - the people and photo reads (V1, V6);
  - a direct `/person/<id>`;
  - a notification body.
- **The plan:**
  - can a third person join, read the thread, or read who is in it;
  - can a plan be made across a block, or with someone out of 1-on-1, by racing two
    accepts or a block against an accept;
  - does `after_tick`'s spread across shared plans write a tick onto a plan the ticker
    is not in?
- **H9's threshold.** Can one person end a plan alone with reports below the threshold?
  Can a report from inside a plan reach the partner in a way that skips H9's count?
- **The brake.** Pulled, can anything still reach a person in 1-on-1? Try every function
  that reads proposals or people (`my_solo_proposals`, `respond_to_solo`, the people and
  photo reads, the export). Can a #8 slip past the drop and arrive late? Can anyone but
  the admin pull it, lift it, or end every plan?
- **The platform switch and the metrics.** The switch is cosmetic by decision: confirm
  nothing relies on it for safety. Does `admin_solo_metrics` let a small gathering's
  counts identify a person?
